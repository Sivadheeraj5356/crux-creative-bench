Creative Bench

A small, honest eval suite for ad-creative analysis agents. Three task families (6 each, seed 7): tag_creative (messaging angle, CTA presence, hook type), detect_fatigue (fatigue only when frequency >= 3 AND CTR down >= 30% from the first-3-day baseline, sustained 2 days - healthy low-frequency dips are traps), pick_winner (two-proportion z-test, alpha 0.05 two-sided; "inconclusive" is a real answer). Fixtures are hand-built and synthetic: they demonstrate the failure modes, they are not production data. Agents see only the task input; scores come back from the grader.
